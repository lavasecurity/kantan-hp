Welcome to your new **Kantan HP** blog! This site is free to run, and you can
edit everything through a browser — no coding, no server, no database.

## How to write your first post

1. Open the editor at **/admin** and log in with your GitHub account.
2. Go to the **Blog** tab and press **New Blog**.
3. Write your title and text, upload a picture if you like, and press
   **Publish**.

Your post goes live on this site within about a minute.

## Where to change site settings

Use the **Settings** tab in the editor to change the site title, tagline, and
theme. Changes appear here on the live site automatically.

Happy writing!
